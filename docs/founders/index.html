The founding standard

What a regional founding shows

A region is recognized as a regional founding when it can show all four of the following.

  1. Three travel-ready players, or a realistic path to fielding that number for team-format preparation.
  2. A named regional coordinator responsible for scheduling and communication.
  3. A brief written doctrine explaining how the group intends to practice, recruit, and prepare.
  4. A commitment to follow the national conduct and reporting expectations.

Responsibilities

What founders commit to

The work is light and it is shared. A region can have several organizers, and no single person owns a region by default.

  • Publish or submit a short seasonal doctrine
  • Keep in touch with the national program
  • Uphold shared conduct expectations
  • Help players understand the captain and roster process
  • Send basic attendance, activity, or readiness updates when asked

Doctrine

Write down how your group plays

Every regional founding publishes a short doctrine. It does not need to be complex. It tells new players what to expect, and it may be featured in showcases and preparation-philosophy rivalries during the season.

Questions

Before you apply

Does founding a region claim that territory?

No. A founding recognizes active organizing work, not ownership of an area. Regional groups are peers, not a hierarchy, and other groups can form nearby or overlap. The shape you draw shows where you play and recruit.

Does founding a region put me on the national team?

No. Regional participation is one of several real entry paths into national player review, and the same standard applies to final decisions regardless of how someone entered. Conduct carries weight equal to skill.

We only have one or two players. Should we still apply?

Yes. The provisional path is for emerging groups that are actively organizing, and the affiliate path is for individuals and small clusters. Both get visibility on the map and access to the process, and both can grow into a full founding later.

Can more than one person run a region?

Yes, and that is encouraged. A region needs a named coordinator for scheduling and communication, and a separate regional selector where practical. Anyone else who helps can be listed as an organizer or contributor.

What happens after I apply?

Organizers review the application against the founding standard and follow up with you on Discord or email. Once your group is recognized, your region goes on the public map and you are connected with nearby groups for scrims.
