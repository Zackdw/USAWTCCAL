How founding works

The standard a regional founding meets, what founders commit to, and when it all happens.

The founding standard

What a regional founding shows

A region is recognized as a regional founding when it can show all four of the following.

  1. Three travel-ready players, or a realistic path to fielding that number for team-format preparation.
  2. A named regional coordinator responsible for scheduling and communication.
  3. A brief written doctrine explaining how the group intends to practice, recruit, and prepare.
  4. A commitment to follow the national conduct and reporting expectations.

Responsibilities

What founders commit to

The work is light and it is shared. A region can have several organizers, and no single person owns a region by default.

  • Publish or submit a short seasonal doctrine
  • Keep in touch with the national program
  • Uphold shared conduct expectations
  • Help players understand the captain and roster process
  • Send basic attendance, activity, or readiness updates when asked

Doctrine

Write down how your group plays

Every regional founding publishes a short doctrine. It does not need to be complex. It tells new players what to expect, and it may be featured in showcases and preparation-philosophy rivalries during the season.

Calendar

Founding runs months one through six

Regions can form, organize, and declare themselves on a rolling basis during months one through six. When the window closes, the national captain election and roster process proceed on stable ground.

Founding window open
Captain election → roster selection → WTC prep
Months 1–6Rolling regional foundings. Form, recruit, scrim, and declare.
End of month 6The founding window closes and the structure locks for this cycle.
Month 7 onwardLeadership and roster decisions proceed on a fixed structure.
